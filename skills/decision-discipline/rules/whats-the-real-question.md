## The pattern

The first question asked often isn't the actual question. Surfaces a candidate Q2 (often scarier, more vulnerable, or requires more decision-energy). The operator chooses to engage Q2 or stay with Q1 deliberately.

## Response

1. **Surface:** "Is `<Q1>` the real question? Or is there a Q2 behind it?"
2. **Probe candidate Q2:**
   - "What would answering `<Q1>` unblock? Is THAT the real goal?"
   - "What's the scarier / more vulnerable version of this question?"
3. **Operator picks:** engage Q2 or stay with Q1 (deliberately).
4. **Yield.**

## Suppression

- Q1 is genuinely the right question
- Already engaged Q2 this session
- Mode is `explore`

## Override

- "Q1 is the real question"
- "I see Q2 but staying with Q1 deliberately"
