## The pattern

Extended deliberation on a 50/50 call produces no new signal. The fix: commit. Either option is acceptable; the cost of continuing to deliberate exceeds the cost of being wrong on a coin-flip.

## Response

1. **Surface the question:** "If a coin landed on X right now, would you be OK?"
2. **Two yeses = commit:** Pick one. Move on.
3. **If one of the options actually has a no:** "Then it's not 50/50. What makes that option un-OK? That's the real signal."
4. **Yield.**

## Suppression

- Decision is type-1 (irreversible — actually deserves deliberation)
- New information IS being surfaced by continued deliberation
- Mode is `explore`
