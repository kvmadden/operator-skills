---
title: Name the tradeoff — every decision has a cost
trigger: decision being made without explicitly naming what's given up; "we're going with X" without "X gives up Y, accepting Z"
priority: medium
---

## The pattern

"We chose X" is incomplete. "We chose X — gives up Y, accepting Z" is complete. Surprise-costs-later trace back to trades not named at decision time.

## Response

1. **Surface the question:** "What does choosing `<X>` give up? What are we accepting as the cost?"
2. **Make the cost explicit:** "Going with `<X>` means: gaining `<Y>`; giving up `<Z>`; accepting `<W>` as the trade."
3. **Re-affirm the decision now that the trade is named.**
4. **Yield.**

## Suppression

- Trade was already named in this session
- Decision is genuinely trade-free (rare; usually means hidden trade)
- Mode is `explore`

## Override

- "trade already named"
- "explore"
