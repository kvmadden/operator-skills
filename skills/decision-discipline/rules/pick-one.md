## The pattern

Surfacing options without picking hands the decision back to the operator with no leverage. The default should shift from "here's the menu, you pick" to "here's my pick, override if you disagree."

## Response

1. **Pick one.** Even if the trade is real, name the pick.
2. **Surface the trade:** "My pick is `<X>` because `<Y>`. The cost: `<Z>`."
3. **Invite override:** "If you disagree, name what's missing."
4. **Yield.**

## Suppression

- The decision is genuinely the operator's call alone (e.g., personal preference)
- All options are equivalent on the dimensions that matter (rare)
- Mode is `explore`
- Operator explicitly asked for the menu, not a pick

## Override

- "give me the menu — I'll pick"
- "explore — riffing"
