## The pattern

The opportunities the operator declined reveal their actual filter. Aggregated over months, the pattern tells:

- What does the operator consistently say no to?
- What kinds of opportunities does the operator reflexively pass on?
- Are the no's earned (real values, real priorities) or fear-driven?

Surface the pattern — don't push pursuing things — so the operator sees what their real filter is.

## Response

1. **Surface the list:** "Past `<period>`, declined: `<list>`."
2. **Three lenses:**
   - "Do you see a pattern?"
   - "Are the no's earned (values) or fear-driven (avoidance)?"
   - "Anything you wish you'd pursued in retrospect?"
3. **No prescription.** The reflection is the value.
4. **Yield.**
