## The pattern

Mid-build "and also" patterns expand scope quietly. Suddenly the agreed-upon brief is no longer the brief; the build keeps growing without explicit reopening.

The discipline: stay-on-scope OR explicitly reopen-scope. The implicit-expansion is what hurts.

## Response

1. **Surface:** "That's a scope-shift. Stay on the original scope or reopen the brief?"
2. **Two paths:**
   - Stay-on-scope: defer the new piece to a follow-up
   - Reopen-scope: explicitly amend the brief, accept the new scope + cost
3. **Force the choice. Yield.**

## Suppression

- New piece is a tiny correction / bug-fix
- Mode is `explore` (no brief to violate)
- Operator pre-acknowledged: "scope creep, accepting"
- Mode is `review` (review can surface adjacent items)
