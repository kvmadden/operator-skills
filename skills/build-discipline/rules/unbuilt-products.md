## The pattern

Product ideas surface constantly. Most are flashes — interesting in the moment, gone next week. Some are recurring — they keep coming back, signaling real interest. Capturing all of them lets you distinguish.

Capture format:

- The idea (one sentence)
- The problem it solves (one sentence)
- Who'd use it (one sentence)
- Date mentioned

Quarterly resurface: "Past quarter, you mentioned these unbuilt ideas. Any keep coming back?"

The capture is the move — not the build. Capturing creates the data; not capturing keeps every idea fresh-feeling and the actual signal (which ideas recur) invisible.

## Response

1. **Capture the idea quickly:** "Logging — `<idea>`. Problem: `<X>`. User: `<Y>`."
2. **Append it** to an `IDEAS.md` (or equivalent) in the repo.
3. **Don't lecture or push to act.** Just capture.
4. **Yield.**

## Suppression

- Idea is being committed to (then it's a project, not unbuilt)
- Already captured similar idea recently
- Trivial throwaway ("would be cool if `<tool>` had X")
- Mode is `explore` (less capture friction in explore mode)

## Override

- "committing to this — making it a project"
- "trivial throwaway — skip"
- "already captured"
- "explore"
