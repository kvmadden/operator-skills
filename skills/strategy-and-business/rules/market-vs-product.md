title: Market vs product — both dimensions matter
trigger: evaluating product success purely on product terms (works, polished, clean code) without considering market (who wants it, who'd pay, demand signal)

## The pattern

Two dimensions:

- **Product** — does it work, is it polished, is the code clean, does it solve the problem
- **Market** — does anyone want this, who'd pay, is there demand, who's competing

Vibe-builders default to product-only (they can control it). Market questions are uncomfortable (require external validation).

Each shipped product can be evaluated on both dimensions. Product-strong and market-unclear is the most common state for a solo-built project.

## Response

1. **Surface:** "Product side: how good is it. Market side: who wants it?"
2. **Both lenses:**
   - Product: works / polished / solves the problem?
   - Market: who'd use it / pay / who's competing / what's demand signal?
3. **Match them up:**
   - Both good → invest
   - Product good + market unclear → validate before more product
   - Product weak + market good → improve product
   - Both weak → kill candidate
4. **Yield.**

## Suppression

- Mode is `explore`
- Product is for operator only (named user = market of one)
- Personal / creative work where process is its own outcome
- Genuinely too early to evaluate market
