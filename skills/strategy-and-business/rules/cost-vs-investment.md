---
title: Cost vs investment — consumed or compounds?
trigger: evaluating a spend / time / commitment without distinguishing cost (consumed, no return) from investment (front-loaded, compounds over time)
priority: medium
---

## The pattern

- **Cost** — time / money / attention spent that's CONSUMED. No future return. Justified by current use.
- **Investment** — time / money / attention spent UPFRONT that compounds returns over time. Justified by future use.

Mixing them up:

- Treating investment as cost → kill it because "it's not paying off yet" — but investments take time
- Treating cost as investment → continue forever waiting for "it'll pay off eventually" — but costs are consumed

## Response

1. **Surface:** "Is `<X>` cost or investment?"
2. **Walk the test:**
   - Will spending here today produce ONLY current use? → cost
   - Will spending here today compound returns over months / years? → investment
3. **Apply the right evaluation:**
   - Cost = current-use math (is this current use worth current spend?)
   - Investment = future-projection-with-uncertainty math
4. **Yield.**

## Suppression

- Genuinely hybrid (some cost, some investment)
- Mode is `explore`
- Time-critical (no analysis room)

## Override

- "skip" or "not now" ends this for the current task. Do not raise it again for the same thing unless something new happens.
