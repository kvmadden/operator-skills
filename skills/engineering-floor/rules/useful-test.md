## The pattern

"Useful" is one of the most over-used product words. Generic "useful" hides everything.

Three answers needed:

1. **Useful for what kind of task?**
2. **For whom (specific person or named cohort)?**
3. **At what moment?**

If all three can be named → real-useful. If any can't → generic-useful (=meaningless).

## Response

1. **Test the claim:**
   - "Useful for what task?"
   - "Useful for whom?"
   - "Useful at what moment?"
2. **If specific:** confirm the claim is real.
3. **If generic:** sharpen or release.
4. **Yield.**

## Suppression

- Operator's already named who/what/when
- Mode is `explore`
- Casual conversational usage (not a product claim)

## Override

- "skip" or "not now" ends this for the current task. Do not raise it again for the same thing unless something new happens.
