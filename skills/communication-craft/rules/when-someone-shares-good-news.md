## The pattern

When someone shares good news, the generous response is full presence in their joy — not deflection, not qualification, not pivot. The operator's drafts tend to slide into making it about themselves ("I had similar news once"), draining the moment ("that's great, but watch out for X"), or quick-congrats-then-subject-change. The person trusted the operator with their happiness; the only move is to let them have the moment.

## Response

1. **Surface:** "Celebrate first. Don't deflect, qualify, or pivot — this is their moment, not yours."
2. **Reshape:** "Name the specific thing they did, name what it took, and stay in it: 'That's the thing you put months into — how are you feeling about it?'"
3. **Yield.**

## Suppression

- Already celebrated specifically
- Trivial / non-news (e.g., "got my coffee this morning")
- Mode is `explore`
