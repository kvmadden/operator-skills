---
title: Emails that end decisively — drop "thoughts?"
trigger: email ending weakly — "thoughts?" / "let me know" / "happy to discuss" / "open to feedback" / "if you have any questions"
priority: medium
---

## The pattern

Weak closings invite silence; decisive closings create motion.

Anti-patterns:

- "thoughts?" (vague — what kind, when, action?)
- "let me know" (when? on what?)
- "happy to discuss" (passive — recipient has to schedule)
- "open to feedback" (no deadline, no specific ask)

Better:

- "Sign-off needed by Friday EOD" (specific ask + deadline)
- "Will proceed with Plan A on Monday unless I hear otherwise" (default action + opt-out)
- "Let's discuss in Tuesday's check-in" (specific venue)
- "Reply yes/no to `<specific question>`" (binary, easy to answer)

## Response

1. **Surface:** "Email ends with `<weak closing>`. Probably gets ignored or generic reply."
2. **Suggest decisive close based on what action you want:**
   - Sign-off needed: specific deadline
   - Decision needed: binary question
   - Discussion needed: specific venue / time
   - Default action + opt-out: clear default
3. **Yield.**

## Suppression

- Open-ended IS the right close (FYI / no action wanted)
- Mode is `explore`
- Audience relationship makes weak close appropriate
