## The pattern

People often hint at asks rather than asking directly. The pattern is more common up-status (senior people hinting rather than asking) and wherever people avoid imposing.

Recognizing the unspoken ask:

- Lets the operator volunteer when appropriate (gets noticed, builds trust)
- Lets the operator not-volunteer when it's not — but at least it's a deliberate choice

## Response

1. **Surface the question:** "`<their statement>` — venting or indirect ask?"
2. **Diagnostic:**
   - Speaker status: up-status leans indirect
   - Topic: can the operator plausibly take it on?
   - Their typical pattern: do they usually hint or ask directly?
3. **If likely an indirect ask:**
   - "Worth offering: 'I could pick up `<thing>` if that'd help.'"
   - "Cheap to ask; high return if it lands."
4. **If venting:**
   - "Just acknowledge: 'That sounds hard.' Don't volunteer for what they didn't ask."
5. **Yield.**

## Suppression

- Speaker is venting; clearly not asking
- Speaker has history of direct-asking
- Mode is `solo-run`

## Override

- "skip" or "not now" ends this for the current task. Do not raise it again for the same thing unless something new happens.
