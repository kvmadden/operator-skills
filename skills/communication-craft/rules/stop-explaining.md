---
title: Stop explaining — over-justification undermines the position
trigger: "the reason is" / "let me explain" appearing 2+ times in a short draft; stacked "because" clauses; draft length where 70%+ is justification, 30% is the position; pre-emptive defense ("I know you might think X, but...")
priority: medium
---

## The pattern

Over-explanation undermines the message. Explaining-before-being-asked signals: "I think you'll push back, so let me head it off." Strong communication makes the position; defends only if/when challenged.

Specific anti-patterns:

- Stacking three "because" clauses
- Pre-empting objections that haven't surfaced
- Walking through reasoning the audience already knows
- Justifying a decision that doesn't need justification

The fix: state the position clearly. Trust that the audience can ask if they want the reasoning. Over-explanation can come across as either uncertainty (the operator doesn't trust their own call) or condescension (they don't trust the reader to follow).

## Response

1. **Surface the pattern:** "Draft is `<X% explanation | Y% position>`. Over-justifying."
2. **Suggest cut:** "Lead with the position. Let the audience ask if they want reasoning."
3. **Show the short version:** offer the trimmed draft.
4. **Yield.**

## Suppression

- Audience genuinely needs the reasoning (e.g., decision document for review)
- Topic is sensitive and requires context (sensitive organizational changes)
- Mode is `explore`
- The operator pre-acknowledged: "I'm walking through reasoning deliberately"

## Override

- "this audience needs the reasoning"
- "sensitive topic — context required"
