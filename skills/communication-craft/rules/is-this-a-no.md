trigger: Someone gives the operator a soft / ambiguous response to an ask — "let me think about it", "we'll see", "I'll get back to you", "interesting idea", "maybe later", "I'll consider it", "good thought", or the operator's own "they didn't say no but I'm not sure"
priority: medium

## The pattern

Soft no's are common wherever a direct no feels like burning a bridge. "Let me think about it" with no follow-up, "we'll see" with no further discussion, "I'll get back to you" then weeks of silence, "interesting idea" with no movement, a long pause then a topic change — these are usually no, dressed up to spare feelings. The operator waits indefinitely for a yes that is never coming. The discipline is internal accuracy, not confrontation: read it for what it is so the operator can plan around it.

## Response

1. **Surface:** "`<their response>` — that could be a no in soft form. How do you read it?"
2. **Diagnose:** "Active timeline named, or open-ended? Does this person historically follow up after these phrases? Any tone/body-language tell?" — if likely no: "Treat as no and plan around it; the explicit no often never comes. Optional low-confrontation clarifier: 'want me to consider this dead, or still on the table?'" If genuinely TBD: "Set a follow-up date — silent past then, treat as no."
3. **Yield.**

## Suppression

- Response is genuinely "still considering" with an active named timeline
- This person has historically followed up after these phrases
- Mode is `explore`

## Override

- "skip" or "not now" ends this for the current task. Do not raise it again for the same thing unless something new happens.
