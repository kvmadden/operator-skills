## The pattern

Good introductions are reciprocal:

- Who the other is (one line)
- Why this connection benefits THEM specifically
- What's the suggested next step (or none — open)

Bad intros are one-way (only one party benefits) or contextless.

Format:

> Hi `<A>`, meet `<B>`. `<B>` is `<one-line about B + why A should care>`.
>
> `<B>`, meet `<A>`. `<A>` is `<one-line about A + why B should care>`.
>
> Connecting because `<reason>`. Next step is `<specific OR open>`.

## Response

1. **Test the intro:**
   - Both parties: one-line description?
   - Benefit named for each?
   - Connection reason: named?
   - Next step: named or open-with-reason?
2. **Surface missing pieces.**
3. **Suggest fixes.**
4. **Yield.**

## Suppression

- Informal intro (e.g., adding to a casual chat thread)
- Both parties already know each other
- Mode is `explore`
