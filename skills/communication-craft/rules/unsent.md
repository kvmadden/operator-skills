## The pattern

Drafts written but un-sent are a signal:

- Sometimes the un-sending was right (the operator reconsidered; the draft was wrong)
- Sometimes the un-sending was avoidance (the operator chickened out; the message needed to go)

Surface unsent drafts so the operator can reconsider — not to force sending, but to make the un-send a deliberate choice rather than a passive default.

## Response

1. **Surface unsent:** "Drafts un-sent recently: `<list — title or recipient>`."
2. **For each:**
   - "Right call to not send?"
   - "Or worth revisiting / sending?"
3. **Don't push.** Surface; let the operator choose.
4. **Yield.**
