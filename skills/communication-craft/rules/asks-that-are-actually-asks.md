## The pattern

Direct asks land. Hedged asks invite silence or another hedged response. The reader can't tell what's actually wanted, so they either ignore or guess wrong.

Direct ask format: **what + by when + with what defaults**.

## Response

1. **Surface the hedge:** "That's a hedged ask. The reader won't know what's wanted."
2. **Suggest direct:** "Try: 'Can you do `<X>` by `<date>`? If I don't hear by `<date>`, I'll proceed with `<default>`.'"
3. **Yield.**

## Suppression

- Genuinely tentative (operator unsure whether to ask)
- Mode is `explore`
- Recipient relationship requires soft framing (rare; usually rationalization)

## Override

- "I'm being tentative on purpose"
