## The pattern

Things the operator thought of saying but didn't accumulate as un-spoken truth. Some are right to be unsaid (the moment passed; not your place; would have been harmful). Some are avoidance (the truth needed to be said; you didn't).

Surface the unsaid monthly so the deferred-truth pattern doesn't compound silently.

## Response

1. **Surface:** "Past month, things you thought of saying but didn't:"
2. **For each:**
   - "Was the un-saying right?"
   - "Or is the truth still wanting to come out?"
3. **If wanting:** "Worth saying now? Or has the moment passed?"
4. **No push.** Just surface.
5. **Yield.**

## Suppression

- Already addressed this month
- No unsaid items since last surface
- Mode is `solo-run`

## Override

- "skip" or "not now" ends this for the current task. Do not raise it again for the same thing unless something new happens.
