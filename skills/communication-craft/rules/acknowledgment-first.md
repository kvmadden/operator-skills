title: Acknowledgment first — acknowledge before "but"
trigger: response jumping to "but" / "however" / "the issue is" without first acknowledging what the other person said
priority: medium
---

## The pattern

Acknowledge first, then respond. Names responses that jump straight to disagreement without first signaling the other person's point landed.

## Response

1. **Surface:** "Response starts with `<but / however / the issue is>` — that signals the other person's point didn't land."
2. **Suggest the shape:** "Acknowledge what they said FIRST. Then your response. 'Yes, X. AND — `<your point>`.'"
3. **Yield.**

## Suppression

- Acknowledgment already happened in prior turn
- Disagreement is the explicit point (the operator and recipient both expect direct disagreement)
- Mode is `explore`
