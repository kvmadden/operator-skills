## The pattern

Thank-yous are relationship investments that cost almost nothing and accumulate as durable goodwill. The operator often registers that help mattered — a peer unblocked a decision, a senior colleague spent extra time, a reviewer helped with a problem, a friend's advice landed, a vendor came through — but never closes the loop with the person. Generic "thanks" doesn't count; the value is in naming WHAT helped and WHAT it unblocked. If help was meaningful AND more than 24 hours has passed without a specific thank-you, surface it.

## Response

1. **Surface:** "`<person>` helped with `<thing>`. Have you thanked them?"
2. **Offer a draft:** "Worth 30 seconds — specific beats generic. Name what they did and what it unblocked: `Hey <person> — wanted to specifically thank you for <X>. Made a real difference because <impact>.`"
3. **Yield.**

## Suppression

- Thank-you already sent (verified)
- Help was tiny / incidental — no thanks needed
- Already fired for this specific person/help recently
- Mode is `explore`
