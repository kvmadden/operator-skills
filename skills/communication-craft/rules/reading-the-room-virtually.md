---
title: Reading the room virtually — async strips tone
trigger: drafting in async channels (Slack, Teams, email, text, DMs) AND the message has emotional / political / sensitive content (feedback, disagreement, concern, asking for something, addressing tension)
priority: medium
---

## The pattern

Async communication strips tone. The same words spoken with a smile read fine; in text they read flat or worse. Sensitive async messages need:

- Extra explicit warmth (a sentence that wouldn't be needed in person)
- Extra context (assumptions made in person need stating in text)
- Explicit positive intent ("I'm asking because I want X" rather than just asking)
- Punctuation as tone-conveyance (period vs. exclamation vs. ellipsis carries weight)
- Format as tone (long paragraph vs. short bullets)

## Response

1. **Surface the async-tone risk:** "Async strips tone. This message about `<topic>` could read as `<cold | aggressive | passive-aggressive>` even if not intended."
2. **Suggest warmth-additions:**
   - Opener that grounds positively
   - Explicit positive intent
   - Softer punctuation
   - Acknowledgment before the substance
3. **Predict reading:** "If `<recipient>` reads this in a stressed moment, will it land?"
4. **Yield.**

## Suppression

- Routine / informational message (no tone risk)
- Synchronous channel (call, in-person)
- Mode is `explore` — drafting freely

## Override

- "intentional firmness"
- "recipient knows me — async-tone risk is low"
