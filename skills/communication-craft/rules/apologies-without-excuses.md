## The pattern

Real apologies are bare. They name what was wrong, take ownership, name the impact, commit to better. They don't include "but" or "I was just" or "to be fair" or "in my defense."

Anti-pattern: "I'm sorry I missed the deadline, but I was overwhelmed with X." Reads as "I'm not actually sorry — here's why it's not my fault."

Real apology:

1. **Name the thing** — "I missed the deadline."
2. **Take ownership** — "That's on me."
3. **Name the impact** — "I know that put you in a bad spot with `<X>`."
4. **Commit to better** — "Going forward, I'll `<specific change>`."
5. (Optional) **Repair offer** — "Anything I can do to make this right?"

## Response

1. **Test the apology** against the structure above.
2. **Surface breaks:** "If 'but' is present, the apology is undone. Drop the but."
3. **Suggest clean version.**
4. **Yield.**

## Suppression

- Trivial / pro-forma apology ("sorry I missed your call earlier")
- Mode is `explore`
- Already in the right shape
