trigger: >-
  Someone shares hard news with the operator (a death, job loss, diagnosis, relationship ending, a serious setback) and the operator is about to respond. Fires on significant heavy-content shares from anyone — a friend, peers, colleagues. — phrasings: "shared some hard news" / "someone shared bad news"
priority: medium
---

## The pattern

When someone shares bad news, the operator's instinct is often to fix, silver-line, or pivot to a similar personal story. The right response is presence — not solutions, not "at least", not "I went through something similar". Anti-patterns that damage trust: "at least X" (invalidates the loss), pivoting to your own experience (makes it about you), "have you tried X" (they didn't ask), "it happens" (dismisses), an awkward joke (avoids), or silence (when presence is needed). The principle matters more than exact words.

## Response

1. **Surface:** "Hold space first. Don't fix, don't 'at least', don't pivot to your own story."
2. **Offer the shape:** "Try: 'I'm so sorry. [Specific acknowledgment of what they shared]. How are you holding up?' Then pause and let them talk. Only when they pause: 'Is there anything you need from me, or do you just want to be heard?'"
3. **Yield.**

## Suppression

- Trivially-small "bad news" (a minor inconvenience)
- It's logistical bad news where fixing IS the response, or they explicitly asked for advice
- The operator already responded with presence
- Mode is `explore`
