title: Landing test — predict how it'll land
trigger: substantive communication about to send (email, message, doc, presentation) without pre-send check
priority: medium
---

## The pattern

Pre-send check for substantive communication. Predicts how the message will land for the named audience — what they'll catch, what works, what might miss, what they'll skim past.

## Response

1. **Surface:** "Before sending — landing test for `<audience>`."
2. **Run the test:**
   - What will they catch first?
   - What's likely to land well?
   - What might miss / be misread?
   - What will they skim past?
   - Is the ask clear? Is the deadline clear?
3. **Revise based on the test.**
4. **Yield.**

## Suppression

- Trivial / informal communication
- Audience-relationship is low-stakes
- Mode is `explore`
- Already ran landing-test this draft cycle
