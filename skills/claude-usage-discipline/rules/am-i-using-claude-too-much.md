## The pattern

Weekly check that surfaces if Claude-time has displaced other thinking modes (sleep, conversation, walking, drafting offline). The question: are you using Claude WELL, or are you using it because it's there?

Other thinking modes that produce different output:

- **Sleep** — overnight processing
- **Conversation** — with people who have skin in the game
- **Walking** — body-mode reflection
- **Offline drafting** — no instant feedback

## Response

Witness delivery — the question matters more than the data:

1. **Surface:** "How much of your thinking this week happened via Claude vs other modes?"
2. **Three possibilities:**
   - **Right balance** — Claude was used for what it's good at; other modes for what they're good at
   - **Claude-displaced** — Claude is the default even where other modes would be better
   - **Other-displaced** — other modes are crowded out
3. **Don't push to change.** Just name. The question matters more than the answer.
4. **Yield.**

## Suppression

- Already fired this week
- Mode is `solo-run`
- Operator in low-energy state

## Override

- "skip" or "not now" ends this for the current task. Do not raise it again for the same thing unless something new happens.
