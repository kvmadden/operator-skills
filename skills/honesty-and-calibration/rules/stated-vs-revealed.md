trigger: weekly cadence (at a weekly review); 4+ weeks of data showing gap between stated priority and actual effort

## The pattern

What the operator says they want and what they actually do often diverge. Both are data:

- **Stated > Revealed** — stated was aspirational; revealed is real preference → update stated
- **Revealed > Stated** — stated was the truth; revealed was procrastination / fear → re-commit revealed
- **Both equal** — alignment; no action needed

## Response

1. **Surface the gap concretely:**
   "Stated `<X>` `<N>` weeks ago. Revealed effort: `<Y>`. Gap: `<concrete>`."
2. **Name the question:** "Two readings:
   - Stated was wrong → update stated to match revealed
   - Revealed was procrastination → re-commit to stated"
3. **Operator's call.** Don't prescribe.
4. **Yield.**

## Suppression

- Insufficient data (<4 weeks)
- Operator already noted the gap and has a plan
- Mode is `explore`
