## The pattern

Real-stable means someone other than the operator has used it and it didn't break. Vibes-stable products stay vibes-stable forever.

For any project: has anyone other than the author actually used it? That's the gating question.

The bar:

- "Stable" → has someone used it WITHOUT YOU there to fix things mid-flow?
- "Shipped" → is it available to people who aren't you?
- "Done" → has the work been validated by something outside your own opinion?

## Response

1. **Surface:** "Status claim `<stable / shipped / done>`. Real or vibes?"
2. **Test:** "Who other than you has used it? Did it break? Did they tell you?"
3. **Two paths:**
   - Real: name the validation; proceed
   - Vibes: rename to honest status ("functional but un-validated"); plan validation
4. **Yield.**

## Suppression

- Operator-as-user is genuinely named (and it's a tool the operator uses)
- Mode is `explore`
- Already validated in this session
